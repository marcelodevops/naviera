## Exact reproduction and measurements

Production URL during verification: `http://127.0.0.1:4321/naviera/`. Measurements were taken after 1.6-1.8 seconds for smooth scroll and 0.5 seconds for menu transition, not during movement.

1. **Mobile anchor label/content:** set viewport to 390x844, load page, click menu button, then Rutas, and wait for scrolling to stop. Section `#rutas` settles at y=-0.078px. Its "Cobertura de rutas" label occupies y=95.922..111.922px, wholly inside the navigation pill y=52..118px. `elementFromPoint` at the label center returns a navigation descendant. The main heading starts at y=127.922px, below the pill, but its first text-line box starts at y=125.922px and the closed-menu strip occupies y=126..144px. Screenshot shows the label missing and the heading's upper portion behind that strip. On desktop the label is y=127.5..143.5px and navigation ends at y=114px: no equivalent obstruction.
2. **Mobile keyboard-focused footer brand:** scroll until the footer's upper border is at the top of the viewport (recorded scrollY=11868). The browser script uses `document.querySelector('footer').scrollIntoView({block:'start', behavior:'instant'})` to make this position exact; ordinary scrolling can reach the same position. Focus the first footer route link, then press Shift+Tab. Focus moves to the footer brand and stays at y=64.922..100.922px, wholly behind the pill y=52..118px; the center hit test returns navigation. Brand text is covered, although parts of the focus outline remain visible around the pill. This is an actual focus/control obstruction, not merely a rectangle overlapping an otherwise empty header region. At desktop, scroll is clamped at page bottom: footer starts at y=512.25px, brand at y=577.25px, and all eight footer links remain below navigation.
3. **Closed mobile-menu focus:** with the menu closed, focus the menu button and press Tab. `document.activeElement` becomes "Rastrear Carga" (`#mobileMenu a[href="#tracker"]`) while its text is clipped. Link box is y=127..171px, but the closed container is only y=126..144px, including padding and border. The screenshot shows a clipped focus outline rather than a readable focused action. The menu opens and all six pointer-selected actions work, but closing it does not remove links from keyboard order. The toggle has no `aria-expanded`; this was measured, not a broad accessibility audit.

The footer's three route links and four service links retain correct order and destinations, skipping the unavailable social/legal spans. At mobile page bottom, copyright y=732.422..748.422px and all three legal labels are visible; route/service/contact links fit the viewport. The tall footer extends above the viewport at bottom, so not every footer element can be simultaneously visible. No horizontal page scrolling was reproduced; animated ticker descendants extend outside their boxes but do not increase document scroll width.

## Source-backed explanation and minimal proposal

- `src/components/layout/Navigation.astro:5` fixes navigation at `top-9` with `z-50`; line 34 gives the closed menu padding, border and margin.
- `src/styles/global.css:64-65` only clips the menu through `max-height:0; overflow:hidden`; closed padding/border still produce an 18px strip, and its links remain focusable.
- `src/scripts/site.js:15-25` toggles the class/icon without hidden/inert state or focus restoration. After selecting a mobile link, DOM focus remains on that now-clipped link.
- `src/scripts/site.js:72-79` scrolls section targets with `block:'start'`, without a fixed-header clearance. `src/components/sections/Routes.astro:5-9` places its mobile label 96px inside that section, still beneath the pill.
- `src/components/layout/Footer.astro:5-9` places its brand 65px inside the footer, so scrolling the footer to y=0 leaves the brand inside the fixed navigation band. Adding general footer padding would not solve arbitrary scroll-position focus obstruction.

**Proposal only, for coordinator/NAV-005 review:** preserve the fixed pill. Add shared anchor-scroll clearance for the closed header (a starting 152px scroll-padding-top covers the measured 144px lower edge with 8px clearance; validate across breakpoints). Make the closed menu genuinely non-rendering/non-focusable, synchronized with its open state, and restore focus to the toggle when closing a selected menu link. For focused content behind the header, use a narrowly scoped focus visibility correction based on the measured closed-header edge, rather than moving the entire footer or treating normal scrolling overlap as a defect. Re-run these exact cases before accepting any fix. These proposals were not implemented or browser-patched and are not proven fixes.
